Tastes Like Metal
Spits Gold

SERIES_SIZE: 8 UNITS // ARTIST: OOAKOSIMO

A collection of eight interactive "Cat Machines." Each piece functions as a looping system—watching, biting, and producing—while never fully resolving.

"A space where critique and complicity overlap."

INTERACTION REQUIRED.
MOVE CURSOR / TOUCH SCREEN TO PROVOKE.
CLICK TO BITE.

ARTIST STATEMENT

I bite the system and it bites back and I sell the bite because rent still exists. This machine critiques capitalism while actively participating in it and that contradiction is not resolved. I made a cat that spits gold every time you provoke it and somehow that felt familiar.
