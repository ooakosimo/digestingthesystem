Flood Control Corruption

What if each sketch is one mechanism of corruption?

An interactive glitch series exploring stolen infrastructure, borrowed futures, and disasters that reveal what bureaucracy hides. Created with p5.js, emphasizing interactivity and dynamic storytelling.

The integrated infrastructure bridges three bodies of work: The Gold Diet, Flood Control Corruption, and Tastes Like Metal : Spits Gold. Together they move from appetite, to machinery, to aftermath—and finally to the body forced to survive it.
